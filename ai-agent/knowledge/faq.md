## Do you have parking facilities?
Yes, all our turf locations feature dedicated, secure parking for both 2-wheelers (motorcycles, scooters) and 4-wheelers (cars).

## Can we bring our own football or cricket equipment?
Yes! You are welcome to bring your own match balls and equipment. We also have standard match footballs available for rent or purchase at the counter.

## Are there changing rooms and showers?
Yes, all turfs have clean changing rooms and restrooms. Thunder Arena and Premier Turf Ground also have fresh water shower facilities.

## Do you have floodlights for evening and night matches?
Yes, all our turfs are outfitted with professional-grade LED floodlight systems ensuring bright, daylight-like visibility for evening and night matches.

## Can we purchase drinking water and refreshments?
Yes! Our on-site shop stocks Kinley mineral water (25 taka), Gatorade / Pocari Sweat sports drinks, energy bars, and cold refreshments.

## Do you provide team bibs and jerseys?
Yes, numbered team training bibs (sets of 5, 7, or 11) can be rented for 100 taka per match or bought brand new from the shop for 250 taka each.

## Can the turf be used for cricket practice or tournaments?
Yes, Premier Turf Ground features a turf layout suitable for tape-ball and soft-ball cricket matches and net practices.

## What is the procedure if a team arrives late?
Bookings strictly adhere to the scheduled time block. If your team arrives late, playtime cannot be extended into the next team's booked slot.

## Can I book recurring weekly slots?
Yes, teams can set up weekly recurring reservations (e.g., every Thursday 8-10 PM) by speaking with management or requesting via WhatsApp.
