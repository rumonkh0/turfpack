## Operating Hours
All turfs operate from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM (23:00) daily, including weekends and public holidays.

## Pricing Structure
- **Base Price**: Standard hourly rate for off-peak hours (morning and early afternoon).
- **Peak Price**: Higher rate during peak demand hours (typically 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM / 10:00 PM).
- **Night Price**: Special rate for late evening slots (9:00 PM onwards).
- **Weekend Multiplier**: Rates are 10-20% higher on Fridays and Saturdays (Bangladesh weekend).

## Payment Methods
We accept:
- bKash (Merchant / Personal transfer)
- Nagad
- Rocket
- Cash (at the turf reception counter)
- Debit/Credit Cards

bKash and Cash are our most frequently used payment channels.

## Booking Policy
- Bookings can be made up to 30 days in advance.
- Minimum booking duration is 1 hour. Slots can also be booked in 1.5 or 2 hour increments.
- A slot is officially reserved once a partial advance or full payment is confirmed.
- Unpaid bookings are automatically cancelled after 6 hours if advance payment is not received.

## Cancellation & Refund Policy
- Cancellations must be made at least 2 hours prior to the booked slot start time.
- **24+ hours in advance**: Full 100% refund or free date/time reschedule.
- **2 to 24 hours in advance**: 50% refund or slot reschedule subject to availability.
- **Less than 2 hours before**: Non-refundable due to lost slot scheduling.

## Weather & Rain Policy
- Games proceed as usual under light rain on covered/well-drained astroturf surfaces.
- In case of severe torrential rain, thunderstorms, or severe weather conditions determined by management, customers receive a 100% refund or credit for an alternative slot.

## Discounts & Packages
- **Block Booking**: Continuous booking of 4 or more hours receives a 10% discount.
- **Frequent Players**: Teams with 10+ bookings get VIP regular priority and discount rates.
- **Corporate & Tournament Packages**: Special day-long or multi-day tournament packages available on request including referee, balls, and trophy ceremonies.
